## Why keep writing separate from projects?

A project page answers:

> What did I build, why, and how?

A technical article can answer a narrower reusable question in more depth.

## Adding diagrams and images

Put the file under `public/images/` and reference it with normal Markdown:

```text
![Description](/images/example.png)
```
